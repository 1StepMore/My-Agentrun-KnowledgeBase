# Bonterms PSA — Additional Terms Playbook

> 原始素材（Bonterms 公开标准合同，CC BY 4.0，抓取 2026-09-24）｜说明：专业服务合同的补充条款谈判手册

## 原文内容

Playbook for Bonterms Professional Services Agreement

 Additional Terms

 

These examples show how to add Additional Terms to a Cover Page for the Bonterms Professional Services Agreement (Version 1.2) (PSA). 

You can delete, replace or add to provisions of the Bonterms PSA.

These are examples only. Have legal counsel review before using. 

 

 PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT (PSA)

 Additional Terms

 The following additions to or modifications of the Bonterms Professional Services Agreement are agreed by the parties and control in the event of any conflicts: 

 Extend Test Period

 Replace the definition of Test Period in Section 3.4(a) with:

 “30 days”

 Provider’s Affiliates Approved as Subcontractors

 Add the following to the end of Section 3.5 (Subcontractors):

 Customer hereby consents to Provider’s use of its Affiliates as Subcontractors. 

 Key Personnel Requirements

 Add a new Section 3.6 (Key Personnel):

 3.6 Key Personnel. Provider will not reassign or remove any key personnel identified in a SOW as such (“Key Personnel”) without Customer's prior written consent, except for circumstances outside Provider's reasonable control (such as resignation, illness or termination). Any replacement Key Personnel will have substantially similar or better qualifications. Provider will ensure a reasonable transition period and knowledge transfer at no additional cost to Customer. For purposes of this provision, Project Leads and Escalation Leads are deemed Key Personnel.

 No Open Source in Assigned Deliverables

 Add as a new Section 5.3 (d):

 Prohibited in Assigned Deliverables. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, in no event will Provider include any Open Source in any Assigned Deliverables.

 Customer Systems – Removal of Provider Personnel

 Add a new Section 8.2(b):

 

 (b) Each party will promptly notify the other when any Provider personnel no longer require access to Customer Systems and Provider will ensure such access is terminated within 24 hours of such notice.

 Customer Policies – General Applicability

 Add as a new first sentence of Section 8.4 (Compliance):

 

 Provider will comply with all Customer Policies applicable to Customer Materials, Customer Systems and Customer Facilities.

 Remove Termination for Convenience of SOWs

 Delete Section 11.2(b) (Termination for Convenience) which reads: 

 

 “Termination for Convenience. Unless otherwise specified in the SOW, Customer may terminate any SOW for any or no reason at any time upon 30 days’ notice to Provider, provided that such termination will not become effective until Customer has paid all fees and expenses incurred through the time of termination.” 

 Indemnity Excludes Open Source 

 Replace Section 13.6 (Exceptions) in its entirety with the following:

 Exceptions. Provider’s obligations in this Section 13 do not apply to claims to the extent resulting from (a) Customer Materials, (b) Open Source elements of Third-Party Materials, (c) Customer’s modifications to the Deliverables or (d) use of the Deliverables in combination with items not provided by Provider. 

 Indemnity Excludes All Third-Party Materials 

 Replace Section 13.6 (Exceptions) in its entirety with the following:

 Exceptions. Provider’s obligations in this Section 13 do not apply to claims to the extent resulting from (a) Customer Materials, (b) Third-Party Materials, (c) Customer’s modifications to the Deliverables or (d) use of the Deliverables in combination with items not provided by Provider. 

 Document Return Certification

 Add to the end of Section 14.1:

 Upon request, recipient will certify in writing its compliance with any return or destruction obligations under this Agreement.
