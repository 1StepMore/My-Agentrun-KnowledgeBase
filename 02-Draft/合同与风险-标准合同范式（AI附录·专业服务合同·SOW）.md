## 核心定义（PSA 原文逐条）

- **Action** — any actual or threatened claim, regulatory proceeding, lawsuit or other legal action.
- **Additional Terms** — any additions to or modifications of the Bonterms Professional Services Agreement that the parties specify on the Cover Page.
- **Affiliate** — an entity controlled, controlling or under common control with a party, where control means at least 50% ownership or power to direct an entity’s management.
- **Assigned Deliverables** — Deliverables specified by the parties to be assigned by Provider to Customer in accordance with Section 6, but excluding any Third-Party Materials or Reserved Materials.
- **Attachments** — any attachments, policies or documents that the parties specify on the Cover Page.
- **Bonterms Professional Services Agreement** — this Bonterms Professional Services Agreement (Version 1.2).
- **Customer** — the party identified as “Customer” on the Cover Page.
- **Customer Facility** — any physical office, workspace, data center or other location used by or under the control of Customer.
- **Customer Materials** — Content or other materials of any kind that Customer makes available to Provider in connection with the Services.
- **Customer Policies** — any information security, privacy, safety, control, protection or other policies or guidelines as Customer may provide to Provider from time to time.
- **Customer System** — any computer, network, application programming interface (API) or other system of any kind that Customer permits Provider to access in connection with the Services.
- **Deliverables** — any Content provided by Provider to Customer in connection with the Services (including any related drafts, interim versions, specifications, flow charts or diagrams).
- **Key Terms** — Effective Date, Rights in Deliverables, Governing Law, Courts or other terms specified by the parties as “Key Terms” on the Cover Page.
- **Laws** — all laws, regulations, rules, court orders or other binding requirements of a government authority that apply to a party.
- **Licensed Deliverables** — Deliverables specified by the parties to be licensed by Provider to Customer in accordance with Section 6, including any Third-Party Materials or Reserved Materials.
- **Open Source** — any open source code or libraries of any type (such as, for example purposes only, any code licensed under any version of the Apache, BSD, Eclipse, GPL or LGPL, MIT or Mozilla licenses).
- **Provider** — the party identified as “Provider” on the Cover Page.
- **Reserved Materials** — any Content of any kind developed by Provider prior to rendering any Services under this Agreement and that is specified as such in the applicable SOW.
- **Services** — the professional services and any Deliverables provided to Customer under this Agreement or any SOW.
- **SOW** — an order or other statement of work for Services that is executed by the parties and references this Agreement.
- **SOW Term** — the term for Provider’s delivery of the Services as identified in a SOW.
- **Specifications** — the description and any other requirements for the Services or a Deliverable set forth in a SOW or this Agreement.
- **Third-Party Materials** — any Content that is not the sole property of Provider and that is specified as such in the applicable SOW.

## 责任限制与赔偿（Limitations of Liability / Indemnification）

> **中文小标题仅便于检索，正文为原文摘录。** 回答「出了事谁担责、最多赔多少、哪些责任不能封顶」——服务合同里最容易吵的就是这一段。

### 责任上限（General Cap）与例外

**原文**：
> *Limitations of Liability. General Cap. Each party's entire liability arising out of or related to this Agreement will not exceed the General Cap. Consequential Damages Waiver. Neither party will have any liability arising out of or related to this Agreement for indirect, special, incidental, reliance or consequential damages or damages for loss of use, lost profits or interruption of business, even if informed of their possibility in advance. Exceptions. Sections 12.1 (General Cap) and 12.2 (Consequential Damages Waiver) will not apply to Uncapped Claims. Nature of Claims. The waivers and limitations in this Section 12 apply regardless of the form of Action, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), strict liability or otherwise and will survive and apply even if any limited remedy in this Agreement fails of its essential purpose.*

**两个定义**（责任限额怎么算）：
> *"General Cap" means amounts paid or payable by Customer to Provider under this Agreement in the 12 months immediately preceding the first incident giving rise to liability.*
> → 中文：**责任上限 = 客户在事发前 12 个月内已付/应付给服务方的金额**（即"12 个月服务费为限"）。
>
> *"Uncapped Claims" means (a) the indemnifying party's obligations under Section 13 (Indemnification), (b) Provider's breach of Section 8 (Customer Materials, Systems and Facilities), (c) either party's breach of Section 14 (Confidentiality), (d) any claims for personal injury or tangible property damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider (including its employees, agents or Subcontractors) and (e) liabilities that cannot be limited by Law.*
> → 中文：**不受上限约束的索赔**（即"封不了顶"的五类）：① 赔偿义务本身；② 服务方违反客户资料/系统条款；③ 任一方违反保密条款；④ 服务方（含其员工/代理/分包商）过失或故意造成的**人身伤害或有形财产损失**；⑤ 法律不允许限制的责任。

### 赔偿（Indemnification）

> *Indemnification by Provider. Provider, at its own cost, will defend Customer from and against any Provider-Covered Claims and will indemnify and hold harmless Customer from and against any damages or costs awarded against Customer (including reasonable attorneys' fees) or agreed in settlement by Provider resulting from the Provider-Covered Claims.*
>
> *Indemnification by Customer. Customer, at its own cost, will defend Provider from and against any Customer-Covered Claims and will indemnify and hold harmless Provider from and against any damages or costs awarded a[gainst Provider] ...*

→ 中文：**双向赔偿**——服务方为「服务方范围索赔」抗辩+赔付（含合理律师费）；客户为「客户范围索赔」抗辩+赔付。具体哪些索赔归哪一方，在 Cover Page 的 Key Terms 里勾选。

### 附带说明：终止后仍然有效的条款（Survival）

> *The following Sections will survive expiration or termination of this Agreement: 2 (Definitions), 6 (Rights in Deliverables), 8 (Customer Materials...), 10 (Fees and Expenses)(for amounts then due), 11.4 (Effect of Termination), 12 (Limitations of Liability), 13 (Indemnification), 14 (Confidentiality), 15 (Required Disclosures), 16 (Publicity) and 17 (General Terms).*

→ 中文：**合同到期/终止后，责任限制（12）、赔偿（13）、保密（14）等仍然有效**——"人走了账还得算"。
